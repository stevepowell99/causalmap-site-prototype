We were really pleased to facilitate a couple of free online sessions with the UK Evaluation Society (UKES).

First up, we hosted "Analysing Qualitative Data with Causal Mapping" on Monday 4 November 2025 (12:30 to 13:30 GMT).

If you're new to causal mapping, or just curious about how it can help you make sense of complex narrative data, this session is for you. We walk through the basics of the method and show how it can help you build a clearer picture of why things change, and how AI can streamline the process.

You can find out more via the [UKES events page](https://app.sheepcrm.com/evaluation/events/evaluation-booking-6881b57abda286fcd1a8b1eb/).

We followed up on 2 December with a session on AI-powered qualitative data collection with QualiaInterviews.
