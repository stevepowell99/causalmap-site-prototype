## Why us

We advise from practice. We build and run AI research tools ourselves: [Causal Map](/product), which uses AI to code causal links in qualitative data at scale with human review, and [QualiaInterviews](https://qualiainterviews.com), which conducts AI-led qualitative interviews. We have worked on qualitative analysis projects with organisations including UNICEF, IFRC, Save the Children, and universities worldwide.

That experience cuts both ways: we know what AI does well in research and evaluation, and we know where it fails, so our advice includes telling you when the answer is "don't".

## How we work

**1. Talk.** A free call about your team, your workflow, and what you hope AI can do for you.

**2. Scope.** We review your workflow together and agree which steps to assist, what success looks like, and how output will be checked.

**3. Build and train.** We set up the tools or pipelines, train your team to run them, and document the method so you can defend it to reviewers, commissioners, and ethics boards.
